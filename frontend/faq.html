Frequently Asked Questions

What does Jai'fore offer?

Jai'fore offers custom printed apparel and merchandise, graphic design, and web development. You can also design your own pieces in the Design Studio, or shop ready-made items.

How do I design my own apparel?

Open the Design Studio, choose an item, upload your artwork (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF), place it inside the print area and check the live preview. Then add it to your cart and check out.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. Create an account with your email and confirm it with the 6-digit code we email you. You can then order, track orders and save designs from your dashboard.

How long will my order take?

Choose a delivery option at checkout. When your items are in stock, Express takes 3–5 business days, Standard 5–8 and No-Rush 8–10, counted from your order. You see the exact dates before you pay. If an item has to be restocked first, Express is not available and delivery takes about 5 more business days.

How much is shipping?

Express is $19.99. Standard is free on physical orders of $60 or more and $15 below that. No-Rush is always free. We ship worldwide from Memphis, Texas, and international orders may take longer because of customs. Web development is by enquiry and has no shipping.

What happens if an item is out of stock?

Some items can still be ordered when we are out of stock. We restock first, so delivery takes about 5 more business days and Express is not available. Checkout shows this clearly, with your new estimated dates, before you pay.

What currency do you charge in, and how do I pay?

All prices are in US dollars (USD). Payment is by card through Stripe, so we never see or store your card details.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel within 2 hours of placing your order for a full refund, as long as production has not started. Email hello@jaifore.org with your Order ID.

What is your return and refund policy?

Every order is custom-made, so our policy differs from standard retail. If something is wrong with your order, email hello@jaifore.org with the subject "Order Issue — [Your Order ID]". We reply within 2 business days, and approved refunds reach your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Read the full Returns & Refund Policy for details.

Will the printed colors match my screen?

Minor color variations can occur between the digital preview and the printed product because of screen calibration and print technology.

Who owns the artwork I upload?

You do. You must own, or have the right to use, any artwork you upload. You give Jai'fore Media a limited license to use it only to produce and fulfil your order.

How do I contact Jai'fore?

Email hello@jaifore.org and include your Order ID if your message is about an order. We reply within 2 business days.

Still have a question?
Email us at hello@jaifore.org and we'll get back to you within 2 business days.
